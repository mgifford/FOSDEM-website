solutions, using a community-powered approach to deliver reliable and
high-performing Linux, AI, hybrid cloud, and container technologies. Red Hat
helps customers develop cloud-native applications, integrate existing and new
IT applications, and automate and manage complex environments. A trusted
adviser to the Fortune 500, Red Hat provides award-winning support, training,
and consulting services that bring the benefits of open innovation to any
industry. Red Hat is a connective hub in a global network of enterprises,
partners, and communities, helping organizations grow, transform, and prepare
for the digital future.
