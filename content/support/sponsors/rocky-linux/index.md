Governed by the Rocky Enterprise Software Foundation (RESF), [Rocky Linux](https://rockylinux.org/) is a
free, open source community enterprise operating system designed to be binary
compatible with Red Hat Enterprise Linux(R). With solid stability, fast
updates, and a 10-year support lifecycle, Rocky Linux is production-ready. From
research and academia to enterprise, industry, and government, thousands of
organizations worldwide rely on Rocky Linux as a basis for their internal and
commercial needs.

Our vision is to create and nurture a community of individuals and
organizations that are committed to ensuring the longevity, stewardship, and
innovation of enterprise grade open-source software that is always freely
available.

We invite all individuals and organizations who believe in this vision to stand
with us and be a part of the Foundation and Projects at <https://resf.org>.

The Rocky Enterprise Software Foundation supports the event financially.
