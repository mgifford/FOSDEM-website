Grafana Labs is the company behind Grafana Cloud, the fully managed
observability platform trusted by more than 10,000 organizations to ensure
reliability, resolve incidents faster, and optimize telemetry at scale.

We were founded on the principles of open source, open standards, open
ecosystems, and open culture. Grafana Cloud, our fully managed observability
platform, is flexible and built for scale, helping organizations run their
software better, accelerate innovation, and move at the speed of their
ambitions. We’re also the team behind some of the world’s most popular open
source projects, including Grafana for dashboards and data visualization, and
our horizontally scalable databases: Loki for logs, Mimir for metrics, and
Tempo for traces.

We are 100% remote with 1,600+ team members in 40+ countries, and we’re backed
by leading investors including Lightspeed Venture Partners, Sequoia Capital,
GIC, Coatue, J.P. Morgan, CapitalG, Lead Edge Capital, OTPP, Sapphire
Ventures, and Tiger Global.

Learn more at [grafana.com](https://grafana.com/).
