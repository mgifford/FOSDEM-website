We believe in open source. Full stop. We started Percona because we’re sick and
tired of rugpulls and watching great companies get held hostage by proprietary
vendors.

Since 2006, we've been building enterprise-grade open source software and
fielding some of the toughest database problems for organizations that refuse
to hand control of their future to a vendor's business model.

We don't do lock-in. We don't do bloated licensing fees. Our focus is
expertise, transparency, and software that's actually yours.

Percona supports the event financially.
