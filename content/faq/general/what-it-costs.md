---
title: "What does organising FOSDEM cost?"
weight: 4
---

We make sure our limited budget is balanced. We derive our income mainly from [sponsorship]({{< relref "/support/sponsors" >}}) and t-shirt sales, plus some [donations]({{< relref "/support/donate.md" >}}). Most of this is spent on making FOSDEM happen. Any profits we invest in making future FOSDEMs even better. Think audio and video gear, taking good care of FOSDEM volunteers, speakers and visitors, and more.
