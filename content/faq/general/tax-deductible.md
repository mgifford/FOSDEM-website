weight: 5

We are a Belgian non-profit without Belgian tax deductible gift status. Tax deductible status is only possible for certain causes. Moreover, a lot of tedious paperwork is required, with no guaranteed success. More info in [nl](https://financien.belgium.be/nl/vzws/giften/erkenning-vzws-stap-voor-stap), [fr](https://financien.belgium.be/fr/asbl/dons/agr%C3%A9ment-asbl-%C3%A9tape-par-%C3%A9tape), [de](https://financien.belgium.be/de/vog/spenden/zulassung-vog-schritt-f%C3%BCr-schritt).
