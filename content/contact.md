## Discuss

There is a [mailing list](https://lists.fosdem.org/postorius/lists/fosdem.lists.fosdem.org/) (and an archive) to help you get in touch with other FOSDEM visitors.

You may also join us for a chat at any time in [the #fosdem:fosdem.org matrix room](https://matrix.to/#/#fosdem:fosdem.org?web-instance[element.io]=chat.fosdem.org).

During the event, you can join real-time discussions and watch talks live on matrix! See the matrix space at [#fosdem-space:fosdem.org](https://matrix.to/#/#fosdem-space:fosdem.org?web-instance[element.io]=chat.fosdem.org). Don't have a matrix account yet? Create one for free at [chat.fosdem.org](https://chat.fosdem.org/#/room/#fosdem:fosdem.org).

## Help Out

Visitors interested in helping out during the event can register for the [volunteers](https://lists.fosdem.org/postorius/lists/volunteers.lists.fosdem.org/) mailing list, where all communication between and coordination of volunteers takes place.

In the months before the event, volunteers can register at [volunteers.fosdem.org](https://volunteers.fosdem.org/) and pick up any of the defined tasks.
