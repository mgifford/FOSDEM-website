title: "Volunteer at FOSDEM!"
---

FOSDEM is a conference entirely made by volunteers. While a small crew works on it year-round, most of the work during the conference is done by a large, enthusiastic, group of great people who donate one or more hours of their time to make FOSDEM happen - **you**. And this year is no different - we need your help. [Sign up here](https://volunteers.fosdem.org/)!
