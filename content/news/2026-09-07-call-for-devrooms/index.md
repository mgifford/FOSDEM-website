Proposals for developer rooms for FOSDEM 2027 can now be submitted!

Developer rooms are assigned to self-organizing groups to work together on open
source and free software projects, to discuss topics relevant to a broader
subset of the community, etc. Most content should take the form of
presentations. Proposals involving collaboration across project or domain
boundaries are strongly encouraged.

We want to continue to focus on promoting collaboration and community between
different projects in niches which don't normally have their own spaces, rather
than large projects with significant corporate backing.

For inspiration, some example rooms along these lines from recent years: Modern
Email, Package Management, and Music Production.

As usual, we unfortunately have a very hard limit on the number of devrooms we
accept due to the realities of the physical space available to us. Every year
we must make difficult decisions about which great proposals must be rejected
(in the past we could have filled the entire conference with every devroom
about a different programming language!). If you submitted a proposal for a
devroom in the last few years and got rejected, don't be disheartened and
submit anyway!

Devroom coordinators must enter a complete schedule into our conference system
by **7th December**. It is particularly important to be available and to
respond promptly to emails about the schedule for about a week either side of
this date. We also require all proposals to be submitted directly by the
speakers into our system.

- Saturday devroom schedules should contain between **7.5 and 8.5 hours** of
  content, beginning at **10.30** and running until between **18.00 and
  19.00**.
- Sunday schedules should contain between **7 and 8 hours** of content and
  start between **09.00 and 10.00** and run through until **17.00**.
- No lunch breaks should be scheduled for devrooms.

If you don't think you can fill a full day, please try to join up with another
project and submit a joint devroom application or indicate on the
[submission form](https://fosdem.org/devroom) that you are requesting only
half-a-day and would like FOSDEM to try to pair you up with another similar
request for half-a-day.

Because our room capacity is limited, we will record and stream all devrooms
live. The recordings will be published under the same licence as all FOSDEM
content (CC-BY).

Devroom submissions will only be accepted with at least two named managers who
are responsible for the room.

If you have questions or need help crafting a good submission, contact us at
[program@fosdem.org](mailto:program@fosdem.org).

The deadline for submissions is **4th October**.

Key dates:

- **04 October**: deadline for developer room proposals
- **20 October**: accepted developer rooms announced
- **27 October** (or earlier): developer rooms issue Calls for Participation
- **07 December** (or earlier): developer rooms publish complete schedules

Note that the [submission form](https://fosdem.org/devroom) should only be used
by organisers of devrooms. If you want to submit a talk to a main track or
devroom, keep an eye on [the website]({{< relref "/news" >}}) until their Call
for participation opens around October 27.
