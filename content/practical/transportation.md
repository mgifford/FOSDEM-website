## By Bus, Metro and/or Tram

Bus, metro and tram lines are provided by the public transport company STIB/MIVB. See the [STIB/MIVB line timetables](https://www.stib-mivb.be/home/travel/realtime/lines) for details.

You can hop on and buy your ticket on board by tapping your debit or credit card. See [tickets and fares](https://www.stib-mivb.be/home/buy).

- **Tram 25**: from Gare du Nord towards Boondael Gare. Stop: ULB
- **Bus 71**: from De Brouckère in central Brussels towards Delta. Stop: ULB
- **Bus 72**: from ADEPS towards ULB. Stop: ULB
- **Tram 8**: from Louise towards Roodebeek. Stop: ULB

## By Train

From Brussels South (a.k.a. "Bruxelles Midi", "Brussel Zuid" or "Gare du Midi") station (about 30 minutes):

1. Take Metro line 2, direction Elisabeth
2. Get off at station Louise
3. Take Tram 8, direction Roodebeek
4. Follow the instructions above.

From Brussels Central ("Bruxelles Central", "Brussel Centraal" or "Gare Centrale") station (about 20 minutes):

1. Take Bus 71, direction Delta
2. Follow the instructions above.

From Brussels North ("Bruxelles Nord" or "Gare du Nord") station (about 30 minutes):

1. Take Tram 25, direction Boondael Gare
2. Follow the instructions above.

From Etterbeek station (about 20 minutes walking), see the [directions from ULB/FOSDEM to the Etterbeek train station](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/ULB+Campus+Solbosch/Etterbeek+railway+station). Trains from Etterbeek to Brussels South take about 25 minutes, but there is only one train an hour. Check the [train timetable](https://www.belgianrail.be/jp/nmbs-realtime/stboard.exe/en) before you leave.

## By Car: Parking

If you come to FOSDEM by car, you can park your car on the campus. The main parking is located between Janson and the K building. The entrance to the parking is on Av. Franklin Roosevelt next to the H building. Note: there is no entrance from Av. Adolphe Buyl.

An additional parking on the campus is available next to the U building at Av. Antoine Depage. Note that this is a one-way street, only accessible from Av. Franklin Roosevelt.

You can park on the campus at your own risk. Do not leave any valuables in your car!

The parkings of the campus can be very busy since early morning during both days. An alternative is to leave your car in the [Parking Sablon-Poelaert](https://www.interparking.be/en/parkings/brussels/sablon-poelaert/), from there to take the Tram 8, direction Roodebeek. Then follow the instructions above.

Please note that most streets surrounding the ULB campus are located in a so-called blue zone. Parking in blue zones is free but limited to a maximum of two hours from Monday to Saturday. A parking disc indicating your arrival time is mandatory. Blue zone signs are placed at the zone's boundaries and are therefore not repeated on every street within the zone. Nearby streets may fall under paid parking regulations, so be sure to check before leaving your car.

The ULB campus Solbosch is within the Brussels Low Emission Zone (LEZ). Drivers of cars registered abroad are required to register before entering the LEZ, or risk being fined. More information can be found on the [LEZ website](https://lez.brussels/mytax/en/).

## By Plane

From Brussels International Airport, located in Zaventem (about 50 minutes):

1. Take the train to Brussels
2. Get off at Brussels Central station
3. Take Bus 71, direction Delta
4. Follow the instructions above.
